# Western local-services marketplaces (US, CA, UK, AU, NZ): UX, trust, monetization, and complaints

Scope: Thumbtack, TaskRabbit, Angi/HomeAdvisor, Nextdoor, Bark, Yelp (Request a Quote), Airtasker, hipages, Checkatrade, MyBuilder, Rated People, Houzz.
Method note: research was done Nov 2026 with web search. Direct page fetches to thumbtack.com, taskrabbit.com and support.airtasker.com were blocked by this session's network policy, so many findings come from search-result extracts of those official pages, not full-page reads. Where sources conflict, both are given. Third-party comparison sites (askbaily, whito, sidequesthustle and similar) have commercial interests, so treat their numbers as rough.

## 1. Task posting flows and how clients compare and choose pros

### Takeaway
Three main models dominate. (a) Lead-gen "request then quotes": Thumbtack, Bark, Angi/HomeAdvisor, Yelp RAQ, hipages, MyBuilder, Rated People. The client fills a guided questionnaire, and the platform routes the request to several pros, who pay per lead or per shortlist. (b) Catalog with upfront hourly price: TaskRabbit. The client describes the task, sees a list of Taskers with their hourly rate, reviews and job count, then picks one and a time slot. (c) Reverse auction or offers: Airtasker. The client posts a task with a budget, Taskers send offers, and the client accepts one, which triggers an escrow charge. The newest trend (2025–2026) is AI intake: Yelp Assistant runs an LLM dialogue and recognizes the job from a photo, and pros reply with AI auto-responders (Yelp Smart Lead Reply, Thumbtack Front Desk).

### Cited Findings
**TaskRabbit (catalog and hourly model)**
- The client compares Taskers by hourly rate, skills and reviews, and sees the rate upfront before booking. TaskRabbit advertises "no hidden fees" and says tipping is optional. Profiles show the hourly rate, number of jobs completed, customer rating and reviews. Clients can filter by price, date and time of day. — [TaskRabbit How it works](https://www.taskrabbit.com/how-it-works); [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/blog/finance/getting-started-with-taskrabbit/)
- Clients can choose same-day help or schedule ahead. Cancelling or rescheduling is free with at least 24 hours' notice. — [TaskRabbit How it works](https://www.taskrabbit.com/how-it-works)
- The TaskRabbit UK blog warns that tasks requested at short notice may carry premium rates. — [TaskRabbit UK blog](https://www.taskrabbit.co.uk/blog/?p=16454)
- The "Elite Tasker" badge goes to Taskers with many positive reviews and many completed jobs. Elite Taskers tend to charge higher rates. — [This Old House review](https://thisoldhouse.com/moving/taskrabbit-review); [Savvy New Canadians](https://www.savvynewcanadians.com/taskrabbit-review/). A "4.8+ rating" threshold appears only on an unofficial 2026 guide and is unverified. — [sidequesthustle](https://sidequesthustle.pages.dev/guides/taskrabbit-guide-2026)

**Airtasker (offers model)**
- The client posts a task, Taskers make offers, and the client reviews each offer with the Tasker's ratings, reviews, badges and completion rate. Airtasker stresses there is "no obligation to accept an offer until you're completely confident". — [Airtasker: How do I choose the right Tasker](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/200956050-How-do-I-choose-the-right-Tasker-for-my-task)
- Completion rate "reflects how often they have successfully finished tasks assigned to them". Some users report that no completion rate shows even after they have finished tasks. — [Airtasker help](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/200956050-How-do-I-choose-the-right-Tasker-for-my-task); [Trustpilot AU](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/www.airtasker.com?page=4)

**Thumbtack (lead model with a questionnaire)**
- Customers submit service requests, and contractors pay to connect with those leads. Pros set lead prices and are charged automatically within a weekly budget. Alternatively, through "Opportunities", a pro is charged only if the customer responds. — [Housecall Pro: What is Thumbtack](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/what-is-thumbtack-how-it-works/)
- Response speed is a ranking and conversion factor. A third-party write-up of Thumbtack's 2025 Pro Summit says pros who respond within 30 minutes are 25% more likely to be hired than those who respond within 3 hours. — [sagnikbhattacharya.com, Front Desk article](https://sagnikbhattacharya.com/blog/thumbtack-front-desk-ai-auto-responder) (secondary source)
- Thumbtack counts response time only during business hours, 8 AM to 8 PM. A lead that arrives after hours starts the clock at 8 AM the next day. — [Thumbtack Community](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/1898/response-time-front-desk)
- "Front Desk" is Thumbtack's automated lead-response service. Pros on the forum debate whether its replies count toward the response metrics. — [sagnikbhattacharya.com](https://sagnikbhattacharya.com/blog/thumbtack-front-desk-ai-auto-responder); [Thumbtack Community](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/comment/6121/)

**Yelp Request a Quote and Yelp Assistant**
- 2020: under the new hiring experience, a consumer requests competing quotes and the best-matching businesses get a "Nearby Job" alert they can respond to. — [Business Wire 2020](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200818005246/en/Yelp-Announces-New-Hiring-Experience-Home-Local/)
- 2024–2025: Yelp Assistant is an LLM chatbot. It "understands the user's issue and engages with them in a dialogue to collect necessary information and match them with businesses". In April 2025 it gained photo recognition that identifies the needed service from an uploaded photo. Yelp says the richer leads let pros "respond with accurate pricing and availability". — [Business Wire, Apr 29 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250429842302/en/yelp-introduces-new-ai-powered-features-to-streamline-hiring-service-pros-and-enhance-the-dining-experience); [Business Wire, Apr 30 2024](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240430179956/en/yelp-announces-spring-product-release-featuring-new-ai-powered-yelp-assistant/)
- Smart Lead Reply is an AI agent that replies instantly to consumers after they submit Request a Quote, "answering questions, gathering project details and qualifying leads". It is reported at $30/month for businesses. — [ACHR News](https://www.achrnews.com/articles/166873-yelp-launches-ai-powered-tools-for-service-contractors); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/yelp-charges-30-a-month-for-ai-agent-that-answers-and-qualifies-leads/)
- Feb 2026: Yelp integrated with Housecall Pro so that leads flow automatically into the field-service software. — [Localogy](https://www.localogy.com/2026/02/yelp-integrates-housecall-pro-to-automate-smb-lead-gen/)

**hipages (AU)**
- The homeowner uploads a photo and describes the job, location and urgency. hipages sends it to local tradies, and the homeowner can get up to 3 quotes and arrange payment in one place. Profiles show star ratings and recommendations. — [Suncorp, article by hipages](https://www.suncorp.com.au/learn-about/maintaining-a-home/questions-when-hiring-a-tradie.html); [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/au/app/id369786244)

**MyBuilder and Rated People (UK)**
- MyBuilder uses pay-per-shortlist: the tradesperson pays when a homeowner shortlists them, and the fee is generally non-refundable. Reported fees range from £2–35 to £15–40 per shortlist, and a job reportedly goes to up to 5 tradies. Rated People reportedly shares a lead with up to 3 trades and charges a subscription plus credits of about £4–25 per lead, or offers an "unlimited leads" subscription. — [whito.co.uk](https://whito.co.uk/plumbers/guides/plumber-lead-platform-costs-uk/); [gasengineersoftware.co.uk](https://gasengineersoftware.co.uk/blog/lead-gen-site-guide-for-heating-businesses/); [askbaily](https://askbaily.pages.dev/vs/mybuilder-vs-ratedpeople) (all third-party)

### Inferences
- Showing the hourly price upfront (TaskRabbit) and holding payment in escrow at offer acceptance (Airtasker) cut "price surprise" for the client. In the lead models, price is unknown until quotes arrive, which explains the complaints about inflated quotes (see section 5).
- Limiting how many pros get each lead (3 on Rated People and hipages, about 5 on MyBuilder) is a key lever for both pro ROI and client spam.
- Response time is both a ranking signal and a tier criterion (see section 4). Platforms reward speed, which pushes pros toward AI auto-replies.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch Thumbtack's customer questionnaire screens (multi-step questions, Instant Book, price estimates shown on pro cards). The site was blocked, and searches found no teardown. Angi's request flow ("Instant Booking", fixed-price "Angi Services") was not verified for 2025–2026.
- No published conversion numbers (A/B tests, growth.design or Baymard teardowns) were found for these platforms.
- Houzz Pro request-and-lead flow: no reliable sources found.

## 2. Reviews, verification, guarantees and escrow/payments

### Takeaway
Guarantees are capped and conditional, and they almost always require booking and paying through the platform. That makes them a tool for keeping transactions on the platform. Verification is layered: ID with biometrics, police or background checks, licence checks, and up to 12 checks at Checkatrade. Airtasker Pay is the clearest escrow model: the money is held in a trust account from acceptance until the client releases it.

### Cited Findings
**Guarantees**
- **Thumbtack Guarantee:** the Property Damage Guarantee goes up to $100,000 and the Money-Back Guarantee up to $1,000, per the official page. Some third-party sites cite $2,500, which conflicts. Damage must be reported within 14 days, and faulty installation and incomplete work are excluded. Customers report denials in which damage was reclassified as "faulty installation". — [thumbtack.com/guarantee (search extract)](https://thumbtack.com/guarantee); [ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/homeowners/thumbtack.html?page=3); [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/thumbtack.com?page=3)
- **TaskRabbit Happiness Pledge:** reimburses eligible claims up to $10,000 per task. It is secondary to the client's own insurance, issues must be reported within 30 days, and TaskRabbit may change or end the Pledge at its discretion. The Pledge page was last updated 8 Dec 2023. — [TaskRabbit Happiness Pledge](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035570011)
- **TaskRabbit Trust & Support fee:** a percentage of the hourly rate that helps fund the Pledge. It does not reduce Tasker pay. In California and Massachusetts, invoices show only a "Service Fee". The percentage is inconsistent across sources (about 7.5% plus a 15% service fee, or 15%). The Fees terms were updated 17 Jan 2025. — [TaskRabbit Trust & Support fee](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/articles/204940570); [Fees supplemental terms](https://support.taskrabbit.com/hc/articles/22213127341197); [toolradar](https://toolradar.com/tools/taskrabbit/alternatives)
- **Angi/HomeAdvisor:** a "Happiness Guarantee" covers homeowners. A 2021 version required reporting within 72 hours. I could not verify current terms or the name "Happiness Check". — [LawnStarter Angi review](https://www.lawnstarter.com/blog/reviews/angi-review/)
- **Checkatrade Guarantee:** a free, discretionary guarantee capped at £1,000 per claim. It does not cover lost deposits. It applies if the client contacted the trade via Checkatrade and paid through Checkatrade Pay. Coverage windows: 12 months for jobs completed before 29 Jan 2026, and 6 months for jobs completed on or after that date. Separate older terms apply to work completed before 26 Jan 2025. The £2,500 figure found is not a consumer payout; it is a liability cap in the business terms. The Book and Pay consumer terms took effect 3 Nov 2025. — [Checkatrade Guarantee terms](https://www.checkatrade.com/guarantee-terms); [Pre-26 Jan 2025 terms](https://www.checkatrade.com/guarantee-terms-pre-26th-January-2025); [Book and Pay terms](https://www.checkatrade.com/book-and-pay-consumer-terms); [Business terms](https://business.checkatrade.com/legal/consumer)

**Verification**
- **Checkatrade:** "up to 12 checks" on each tradesperson plus ongoing monitoring, and a Primary Authority partnership with Trading Standards. A Guardian investigation (Nov 2025) found a rogue trader who slipped through. Checkatrade blamed a "rare human error" that omitted 2 of the 12 checks, and the customer was paid £1,000 under the guarantee. — [Checkatrade trust blog](https://www.checkatrade.com/blog/trust/); [Guardian via inkl](https://inkl.com/news/how-do-rogue-traders-get-past-checkatrade-s-checks)
- **Airtasker:** the ID Verified badge requires a government photo ID plus a live biometric face scan (since Oct 2024). In Australia this is a Digital ID badge verified through Australia Post. The Police Check badge is given only with a clean police record ("no Disclosable Court Outcomes"). The US has a Background Check badge run by Checkr, and the UK has Gas Safe-related badges. Badges are optional, and a Tasker can be licensed without showing a badge. — [Airtasker badges help](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/115010428848); [Licensing help](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/360025184612-How-do-I-know-if-a-Tasker-is-qualified-or-licensed-to-do-the-work)
- **Thumbtack:** pro profiles can show background-check status, and community answers link the check to eligibility for rewards. — [Thumbtack safety guidelines](https://www.thumbtack.com/safetyguidelines/); [Jobber](https://getjobber.com/academy/thumbtack-for-contractors/)
- **Checkatrade:** advertises 4.6/5 on Trustpilot from 75,000+ verified reviews. A third-party page gives 4.8 from 114,000+, which conflicts. — [Checkatrade about](https://www.checkatrade.com/about)

**Escrow and payments: Airtasker Pay**
- When the client accepts an offer, the task price plus a connection fee is charged to a card and held in an Airtasker Trust Account until completion. The client releases the funds after the work is done. The Tasker receives the payment minus a service fee. — [Airtasker Payment Guidelines](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-us/articles/225553508-What-are-the-Payment-Guidelines); [Why was my card charged](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-au/articles/115010433128-Why-was-my-card-charged-before-the-start-of-the-task)
- The client connection fee runs from AUD 9.95 to a maximum of AUD 59.95 depending on the task price. It is waived for customers with an Airtasker membership. — [Airtasker pricing help](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020792111-What-are-the-things-I-need-to-know-about-pricing-and-payments); [Airtasker membership](https://www.airtasker.com/au/lp/airtasker-membership/)
- On cancellation, the held funds go back to the card or become Airtasker credit; sources differ. Refunds take 5–7 business days. An older page says payouts reach the Tasker within 3–5 business days. — [Airtasker help](https://support.airtasker.com/hc/en-us/articles/205832680)

### Inferences
- A typical pattern: the guarantee applies only to "booked and paid via platform" jobs. That ties the trust feature to retention of the transaction and fights off-platform leakage (Checkatrade Pay, Thumbtack, TaskRabbit).
- Checkatrade cut its window from 12 months to 6 in Jan 2026, which suggests guarantee costs are a real pressure. Caps are modest relative to job values, except Thumbtack's $100k property-damage cap.
- Optional badges (Airtasker) put the verification cost on the provider but reward them with conversion. "Badge absent ≠ bad" messaging is needed to avoid unfairness.

### Gaps
- Review mechanics were not verified: who can review (only booked customers?), review prompts and timing, and whether pros can publicly reply. I found no official Thumbtack, TaskRabbit or Angi help pages in search results.
- The current Angi guarantee name and terms for 2025–2026 are unconfirmed.

## 3. Messaging, notifications, rebooking, scheduling

### Takeaway
I found little hard evidence here. What I confirmed: TaskRabbit lets clients book a specific Tasker in a chosen time slot, same-day or scheduled, with free cancellation 24h ahead. Messaging is increasingly automated on the pro side (Yelp Smart Lead Reply, Thumbtack Front Desk), and lead platforms push integrations into pros' scheduling software (Yelp with Housecall Pro).

### Cited Findings
- TaskRabbit: same-day or scheduled booking, filtering by date and time of day, and free reschedule or cancel with at least 24h notice. — [TaskRabbit How it works](https://www.taskrabbit.com/how-it-works)
- Yelp Smart Lead Reply gives instant AI replies after Request a Quote. — [ACHR News](https://www.achrnews.com/articles/166873-yelp-launches-ai-powered-tools-for-service-contractors)
- Yelp leads integrate with Housecall Pro (Feb 2026). — [Localogy](https://www.localogy.com/2026/02/yelp-integrates-housecall-pro-to-automate-smb-lead-gen/)
- Nextdoor's 2025 redesign uses AI to turn "14 years of conversations" into a recommendation engine called "Nextdoor Faves". Separately, the annual Neighborhood Faves awards let neighbors vote by Faving a Business Page, recommending it, or @mentioning it, across 20 categories. Nextdoor claims winners get 32x more Business Page views. — [Nextdoor blog: Meet the new Nextdoor](https://blog.nextdoor.com/meet-the-new-nextdoor); [Introducing Faves](https://blog.nextdoor.com/introducing-faves-harnessing-the-collective-wisdom-of-your-neighborhood); [Nextdoor Fave Awards 2026](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/small-business/fave-awards); [Nextdoor business blog](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/blog/how-to-make-the-most-of-nextdoor-neighborhood-favorites); [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/nextdoor-rolls-out-massive-redesign-with-ai-recommendations-and-emergency-alerts/)

### Inferences
- Nextdoor's distinctive approach is social proof from mined neighbor conversations, with no paid lead. Recommendations come from @mentions and posts, not from a structured review form.

### Gaps
- No sourced details on "favorite Tasker" or rebook flows (TaskRabbit, Airtasker), in-app chat rules (phone masking, bans on sharing contacts), or notification cadence. These could not be verified because the official help centres were blocked.

## 4. Gamification and provider tiers

### Takeaway
Tiers are based on rating, review volume, response speed and completion. They are re-evaluated periodically, they gate a visible badge, and they cover a small share of pros (Thumbtack Top Pro is about 4%).

### Cited Findings
- **Thumbtack Top Pro:** about 4% of pros, selected periodically. Older Pro Center text says twice a year; newer sources say reevaluation happens 4 times a year. Criteria include high ratings, at least 5 verified reviews in 12 months (2018 source), a background check, and responsiveness. The response threshold conflicts: 75% within 4 business hours, or within 24 hours. — [Thumbtack Pro Center](https://pro-center.thumbtack.com/?p=19905); [Jobber](https://getjobber.com/academy/thumbtack-for-contractors/); [Thumbtack Community](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/2075/top-pro-status)
- **Thumbtack Pro Rewards (2024–2025):** points-based tiers up to Silver, Gold and Platinum, with quarterly rewards periods. Per moderators, the badge next to a pro's name shows only while they hold Platinum. An annual badge requires qualifying in at least 2 periods and appears in January of the next year. A secondary source cites response-rate thresholds of 65% (Gold) and 75% (Platinum) of leads answered within 1 hour. — [Thumbtack Community: Top Pro Badge 2024](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/comment/5820/); [Silver tier post](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/2216/how-some-pros-hit-the-silver-pro-rewards-tier-in-their-first-week); [sagnikbhattacharya.com](https://sagnikbhattacharya.com/blog/thumbtack-front-desk-ai-auto-responder)
- **Airtasker:** "Top Tasker" badges are awarded on average rating, reviews and completion rate, alongside the verification badges above. — [Airtasker Badges blog](https://airtasker.com/blog/?p=2227) (updated 2021, older)
- **TaskRabbit Elite Tasker:** awarded for many positive reviews plus high job volume. — [This Old House](https://thisoldhouse.com/moving/taskrabbit-review)
- **Nextdoor Neighborhood Faves:** an annual award with a window sticker, a trophy badge on the Business Page, and a featured spot in the Local Faves hub. — [Nextdoor Fave Awards](https://business.nextdoor.com/en-us/small-business/fave-awards)

### Inferences
- Tier rules are opaque, and pros on the forums are confused about them (conflicting thresholds, when the badge appears), which hurts motivation. Clear, published thresholds plus progress meters are a likely improvement.

### Gaps
- Checkatrade's numeric score system (reviews scored out of 10 on several sub-criteria) was not confirmed in this round's sources.

## 5. Top complaints (clients and providers), regulatory action, ratings

### Takeaway
The dominant provider complaint across all lead-gen platforms is paying for leads that are fake, unresponsive, already hired, or shared with too many competitors, along with difficult credit refunds and auto-renewals. HomeAdvisor was fined by the FTC for misrepresenting lead quality. Client complaints focus on guarantee denials, inflated or distant quotes, and fees added at checkout.

### Cited Findings
- **FTC v. HomeAdvisor:** the complaint was filed March 2022. The final order (Jan 2023) totals up to $7.2M: $4,448,000 to the FTC plus $2,752,000 to an mHelpDesk redress fund. HomeAdvisor was barred from false claims about the source and quality of leads, including claims that leads came from its own website. HomeAdvisor admitted no wrongdoing. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/01/23/the-ftc-fines-homeadvisor-up-to-7-2m-for-lying-about-lead-quality-and-other-matters); [Subscription Insider](https://www.subscriptioninsider.com/topics/regulation-and-compliance/ftc-finalizes-order-against-homeadvisor-for-deceptive-marketing); [FTC consent order PDF](https://Www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/d09407homeadvisorconsentorder.pdf)
- **Bark:** credits per lead vary from 3 to more than 20. Sellers say leads cost about £12 each, up from earlier years. Common complaints are fake leads, unresponsive contacts, and "tyre kickers" who already hired someone. Leads must be reported within 14 days to qualify for a credit return. Bark replies that "all our leads are from genuine customers… we cannot control if a lead is unresponsive". A late-2025 move to a "Marketplace" subscription model triggered complaints about paid credits expiring and automated charges. Bark has about 109k Trustpilot reviews. — [UK Business Forums](https://www.ukbusinessforums.co.uk/threads/experience-with-bark-com.373033/post-2872716); [G2](https://g2.com/products/bark-com/reviews?page=10); [Trustpilot AU](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/bark.com?page=5); [ProductReview.com.au](https://www.productreview.com.au/listings/barkcom)
- **Thumbtack (pros):** reported lead costs are $35–75 per lead, and one pro paid $120 for a lead on a $400 job. Pros complain about paying for leads that never reply. BBB lists 1,037 complaints in 3 years. Thumbtack's "Opportunities" (charged only if the customer responds) partly addresses this. — [ConsumerAffairs](https://consumeraffairs.com/homeowners/thumbtack.html); [BBB](https://prod.bbb.org/us/ca/san-francisco/profile/internet-service/thumbtack-inc-1116-367066/complaints?page=99); [Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/what-is-thumbtack-how-it-works/); [Thumbtack Community: "If customers aren't responding, what is Thumbtack selling?"](https://community.thumbtack.com/discussion/comment/7660/)
- **MyBuilder:** Trustpilot complaints centre on non-refundable shortlist fees for fake or dead leads, with jobs shared among up to 5 tradies. Reviews are mixed, and clients praise work quality. — [Kimola analysis of MyBuilder Trustpilot reviews](https://kimola.com/reports/in-depth-mybuilder-feedback-analysis-report-trustpilot-en-gb-145802); [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.mybuilder.com?page=8)
- **Rated People:** auto-renewing contracts and disputes over cancellation billing. — [whito.co.uk](https://whito.co.uk/trades/research/uk-trade-directory-costs/) (third-party)
- **hipages:** tradies say people post jobs just to price them, so tradies pay for non-buyers. Clients complain that quotes are "ridiculously high" or come from tradies "the other side of town". ProductReview advises clients to check licences and reviews independently. — [ProductReview.com.au](https://www.productreview.com.au/listings/home-improvement-pages); [Trustpilot](https://ch.trustpilot.com/review/hipages.com.au?page=10)
- **TaskRabbit (clients):** confusion over the Trust & Support fee shown at the final booking step. TaskRabbit's Trustpilot replies point to the final booking page where the fee is disclosed. — [Trustpilot](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/taskrabbit.com?page=8); [ToS Watchdog](https://terms.law/ToS-Watchdog/home-services/taskrabbit/)
- **Checkatrade:** rogue traders pass vetting in rare cases (Guardian, Nov 2025), and City AM quotes Checkatrade saying "more can be done to protect customers from rogue traders". — [inkl/Guardian](https://www.inkl.com/news/does-checkatrade-s-vetting-really-check-out); [City AM](https://www.cityam.com/?p=2309053)

### Inferences
- Charge-on-engagement (Thumbtack Opportunities, MyBuilder shortlist) is the pro-friendly evolution of pay-per-lead, because it moves the risk of an unresponsive client away from the pro. Even so, complaints persist when the client shortlists without intent to hire.
- Regulatory risk is real for lead-quality claims (the FTC case). Transparent lead-source labelling and easy refund flows are both trust and compliance features.

### Gaps
- No current App Store or Google Play rating numbers were collected (blocked or not surfaced). Trustpilot review counts were found (Bark ~109k, TaskRabbit ~57k, MyBuilder ~56k, Airtasker ~15k, Thumbtack ~6.5k), but not consistent star averages.
- No published UX research or conversion case studies (growth.design, Lenny's, Baymard) specific to these marketplaces were found within the search budget.
- No sourced no-show statistics.
