Research date: 2026-11-15. Method: web search, about 17 tool calls. Most primary pages could not be fetched because the session network blocked these hosts: woshipm.com, careers.daangn.com and redbusbagman.com. ## 1. Managed service marketplaces: Urban Company's fixed pricing, trained pros and warranty compared with open marketplaces; 58到家 standardization

### Takeaway
Urban Company (UC) and 58到家 follow a "managed marketplace" model. The platform sets the price, vets and trains workers in its own classrooms, and backs the job with a damage cover and service guarantee. Open directories like Justdial and Sulekha only sell leads. The managed model brings higher repeat use (UC: about 77% of revenue from repeat customers, unverified) and profitability (UC FY25 net profit ₹239.76 cr). The cost falls on workers, who complain about fees, ID blocking and "instant" 15-minute formats.

### Cited Findings
**Urban Company (India, also UAE, Singapore and Saudi Arabia)**
- Fixed prices are shown before booking. Refrigerator repair pages list fixed prices for genuine spare parts plus a **flat labour charge of Rs 249**. Electrician pages say the price for the electrician's time is listed in the app and on the website before booking. — [Urban Company refrigerator repair page](https://urbancompany.com/bangalore-refrigerator-repair-motappapalya-indiranagar); [UC electricians page](https://urbancompany.com/bangalore-electricians)
- Professionals are described as "background-verified & trained ... **4.3+ rated**". This implies a rating floor for pros who stay on the platform. — [UC refrigerator repair page](https://urbancompany.com/bangalore-refrigerator-repair-motappapalya-indiranagar)
- Protection: "**up to Rs 10,000** against damages (no insurance on cash payment)", plus a service guarantee. Some pages mention a "UC warranty & damage cover". The trust bar is branded "UC Promise" with the labels Verified Professionals, Hassle-Free Booking and Transparent Pricing. The full UC Promise terms were not found. — [UC Hyderabad electricians page](https://urbancompany.com/hyderabad-electricians); [UC Pune refrigerator page](https://urbancompany.com/pune-refrigerator-repair-kalyani-nagar)
- Training in the IPO RHP: in-house training plus access to tools and consumables. The training team reportedly runs **247 classrooms across 17 cities** (339 staff). Other sources say "175+ rooms across 14 cities", so the figures conflict. Onboarding includes background checks and skill assessments. — [Upstox, RHP qualitative factors](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/ipo/why-urban-company-nearly-74-on-listing-day-key-qualitative-factors-from-the-rhp/article-181426/); [Upstox RHP article 2](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/ipo/urban-company-ipo-a-look-at-its-qualitative-factors-and-financials-from-rhp/article-181068/)
- Financials: FY25 revenue from operations was **₹1,144.46 cr (+38.2% YoY)**, and net profit was **₹239.76 cr**, against a loss of ₹92.77 cr in FY24. The average service-professional rating was **4.79/5** in the quarter ending 30 June 2025. A claim that **77% of revenue comes from repeat customers** is not verified against the filing. The IPO ran 10–12 Sept 2025 at a ₹98–103 price band, and the shares rose about 74% on listing day. — [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/ipo/why-urban-company-nearly-74-on-listing-day-key-qualitative-factors-from-the-rhp/article-181426/); [ICICI Direct IPO review](https://www.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_urbancompany_iporeview.pdf); [StockAxis IPO note](https://stockaxis.com/IPO/Urban-Company-Limited-100925015809.html)
- "Insta Maids", a 15-minute home-help service, launched in March 2025. It was renamed "**Insta Help**" after backlash that the name undermined domestic workers' dignity. Gig-worker unions (IFAT and the Telangana Gig and Platform Workers Union) called it exploitative. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/urban-company-rebrands-insta-maids-to-insta-help-amid-backlash-59682.htm); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/start-up/news/urban-company-renames-insta-maids-to-insta-help-after-backlash); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urban_Company)
- Worker-side complaints: no time for meals between bookings, and a helpline that did not help in an emergency. UC pointed to its SOS button, 24/7 partner helpline and women-only safety helpline. Al Jazeera (March 2024) described a "pay-to-work" model with training fees and product purchases, and said earnings fell for women beauticians. — [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/jobs/urban-company-insta-maids-help-domestic-workers-in-15-minutes-women-safety-minimum-wages-dignity-labour-latest); [Al Jazeera 2024](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2024/3/1/how-indias-urban-company-has-soured-gig-work-for-women)

**58到家 (China; data mostly from 2022, may be outdated)**
- Strategy of "标准化 + 数字化" (standardization plus digitization). Merchants must pass enterprise qualification checks, pay a **security deposit**, complete training and pass an **online exam** to be "持证上岗" (certified before taking jobs). — [China Daily CN, 2022-03](https://cn.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202203/16/WS6231abc9a3101c3ee7acbf44.html); [21财经, 2022-03](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220318/herald/1b68ed9a9d31cab4b0bfbf3403151527.html)
- Worker entry has **13 screening criteria**: age, health check at a grade-2A or higher public hospital, background check, experience, certificates and others. Cleaners take **10 courses / 80 class hours**. Only **1 in 100** applicants starts working. Acceptance checks are standardized, for example: no dust on furniture, no grease on the hood or stove, clean and shiny windows (8 items). — [China Daily CN](https://cn.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202203/16/WS6231abc9a3101c3ee7acbf44.html); [Sina 2022](https://news.sina.cn/sx/2022-03-29/detail-imcwiwss8736149.d.html)
- Dispatch is tied to a score. Workers are scored on customer satisfaction, rejection rate and willingness to take orders. In a 2022/23 modelling competition built on 58到家 data, the worker "service score" ranged over [0,1] and was derived from historical order ratings. An older interview describes **star levels**: for example, serving 300 customers who all gave a 9/10 earns promotion to 2-star. — [21财经](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220318/herald/1b68ed9a9d31cab4b0bfbf3403151527.html); [CSDN modelling task](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_58446570/article/details/128684643); [高顿 / Yao Jinbo interview](https://m.gaodun.com/caiwu/557925.html)
- Business-model commentary: "58到家的生意，既苦又累，而且还慢" ("hard, tiring and slow"). Standardization is costly and growth is slow. — [PingWest](https://www.pingwest.com/a/192596)
- Old customer complaint (Sina blog, roughly 2015): two unpleasant cleaning experiences. This is an anecdote. — [Sina blog](https://blog.sina.com.cn/s/blog_69680a460102vf2b.html)

**Open lead-gen directories (Justdial, Sulekha) for contrast**
- Justdial business users complain about fake or low-quality leads. One reviewer said they got 200+ "leads" in a year that were almost all fake (invalid numbers, wrong business names). Others report sales calls about twice a week pushing paid promotion, and spam calls and emails. Trustpilot's summary also notes that many reviewers find Justdial's leads useful. — [Trustpilot Justdial](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/justdial.com); [Voxya complaint](https://voxya.com/consumer-complaints/fake-leads-and-calls-scam-extortion-through-ecs/223121); [ConsumerComplaints.in](https://www.consumercomplaints.in/justdialcom-b100558)
- Sulekha: reviews complain of robot and marketing calls, SMS and WhatsApp spam, and fake enquiries or useless paid leads. — [Trustpilot Sulekha](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/localservices.sulekha.com)

### Inferences
- In the managed model, the platform takes over price setting, quality control and liability (fixed price, a training funnel, damage cover up to a set amount, a rating floor). Open directories leave all three to the user and earn money by selling leads to businesses. The lead-gen model invites complaints about lead quality and spam calls.
- Both UC and 58到家 tie dispatch or visibility to ratings, rejection rate and similar metrics. This is a quality lever, but it is also the main source of worker grievances (ID blocking, pressure).

### Gaps
- No official UC warranty period or claims procedure (UC Promise terms) was found. No UC NPS figure was found. App Store and Google Play ratings for UC were not retrieved.
- No 2025–2026 data for 58到家. Meituan's merchant rating formula and star rules were not found in available sources.
- Kaodim (Malaysia) and Gojek GoLife were not researched within the tool budget. From background knowledge, GoLife was shut down in 2020; this should be checked before use.

## 2. Trust mechanisms: Xianyu and Sesame Credit, Karrot neighborhood verification, Mercari escrow and anonymous shipping, Carousell verification and protection

### Takeaway
Each platform builds trust around its own local infrastructure. Xianyu uses Alipay and Zhima credit scores (prepayment for sellers with a score of 600+). Karrot uses GPS neighborhood checks and a social score. Mercari uses escrow plus anonymous shipping through Japanese carriers. Carousell uses Singapore's national ID system, Singpass, via verified badges. Dubizzle and Jiji use document or selfie checks and in-person store visits. The weak spot is that badges get sold or hijacked, as Carousell's 2025–2026 case shows.

### Cited Findings
**Xianyu / 闲鱼 / Goofish (Alibaba)**
- **信用速卖** ("credit quick-sell", launched 2017) is a C2B recycling flow through third-party partners. A user with a **Zhima score above 600** gets a **prepayment** immediately, sized by score, and the balance is settled after the partner inspects the item. The general principle is "先收钱，再发货" (get paid first, then ship) for 600+ users. — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/1722103578625); [ifanr](https://www.ifanr.com/754716); [mydrivers](https://news.mydrivers.com/1/912/912019.htm)
- Zhima/Sesame Credit is Ant Group's private credit score, built from Alibaba-ecosystem data. — [Wikipedia: Zhima Credit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhima_Credit)
- Monetization: Xianyu announced a **0.6% basic "software service fee"** for all sellers, **capped at RMB 60** per transaction. For professional sellers on **闲鱼小铺** the rate rises to **1.6%** and the cap is removed. A 2023 plan to charge high-frequency sellers above a threshold preceded this. Baidu Baike gives no year for the notice; tech press dates it to 2024, which is not confirmed here. — [Baidu Baike EN: Idle Fish](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Idle%20Fish/14540)
- Xianyu is described as a "consumer barometer" in China's down-trading economy, and as a significant second-hand luxury channel. — [Dao Insights](https://daoinsights.com/news/goofish-economy-why-is-the-online-marketplace-a-consumer-barometer/); [Jing Daily](https://jingdaily.com/secondhand-luxury-platform-profile-alibabas-idle-fish/)

**Karrot / Danggeun / 당근 (Korea; also Japan, Canada, UK and US)**
- **Manner temperature (매너온도)**: new users start at **36.5°C** (normal body temperature), shown in blue. The range is **0–99°C**. The score combines manner ratings from trade partners, reviews and sanctions. **The last 1 year** of data counts, and more recent reviews weigh more. A personal blog says 3 years; the official-press version (1 year) is more credible. — [Newstap](https://www.newstap.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=184345); [Nate News 2021](https://news.nate.com/view/20210618n12520); [ngetnews](https://www.ngetnews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=520183)
- Community posts say the score drops with bad-manner ratings and policy violations, and **drifts down slowly with inactivity**, possibly below 36.5. Other cited factors include activity level and reply speed. The exact weights are not public. — [Daangn community Q&A](https://www.daangn.com/kr/community/%EB%8B%B9%EA%B7%BC-%EB%A7%A4%EB%84%88-%EC%98%A8%EB%8F%84%EA%B0%80-36-5-%EB%B3%B4%EB%8B%A4-%EB%82%AE%EC%9D%80-%EA%B2%BD%EC%9A%B0%EB%8A%94-%EC%96%B4%EB%96%A4-%EA%B2%BD%EC%9A%B0%EC%9E%85%EB%8B%88%EA%B9%8C-8f6q9cxpvyvu/); [online-financer blog](https://online-financer.com/%EB%8B%B9%EA%B7%BC-%EC%98%A8%EB%8F%84-%EC%98%AC%EB%A6%AC%EA%B8%B0/)
- Gamified culture: users treat temperature bands as personality types ("재미로 보는 당근온도별 특징", "traits by Karrot temperature, for fun"). The press published league tables of neighborhoods with the most users at the 99° maximum. — [Daangn community](https://www.daangn.com/kr/community/%EC%9E%AC%EB%AF%B8%EB%A1%9C-%EB%B3%B4%EB%8A%94-%EB%8B%B9%EA%B7%BC%EC%98%A8%EB%8F%84%EB%B3%84-%ED%8A%B9%EC%A7%95-tx1s8m5nwiy5/); [Newstap](https://www.newstap.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=184345)
- **Localization lesson:** in global markets the metaphor did not translate. Karrot first renamed it "Neighbour Meter" and then replaced it with a **"Karrot Score" with a base of 30**, not 36.5 (UX-research write-up). Daangn's own engineering blog covers this under the title "쿨한 사람? 따뜻한 사람? 매너온도 글로벌 수출기" ("A cool person? A warm person? Exporting manner temperature globally"). The full text could not be fetched. — [redbusbagman Karrot UX research (search summary)](https://redbusbagman.com/karrotuxresearch/); [Daangn careers blog](https://careers.daangn.com/blog/post/%EB%8B%B9%EA%B7%BC%EB%A7%88%EC%BC%93-%EB%A7%A4%EB%84%88%EC%98%A8%EB%8F%84-%ED%95%B4%EC%99%B8%EC%8B%9C%EC%9E%A5-%EC%A7%84%EC%B6%9C-1/)
- Fraud pressure: the press reports recurring trade scams and Karrot "staking everything" on protecting the manner-temperature system. — [ngetnews](https://www.ngetnews.com/news/articleView.html?idxno=520183)

**Mercari (Japan / US)**
- **Anonymous shipping**: Mercari's own careers blog says that in conventional C2C services buyers and sellers saw each other's addresses. Fear of being traced after a dispute led Mercari to build anonymous shipping, in which carrier-issued codes or labels hide names and addresses. — [Mercari careers / Mercan](https://careers.mercari.com/en/mercan/articles/59470/)
- **Escrow**: payment is held until the buyer receives and **rates** the item, and the rating releases the funds. Guides warn that rating too early forfeits refund eligibility. In the US, a 3-day inspection window after delivery is cited by third-party guides, not official sources. — [McAfee](https://www.mcafee.com/learn/what-is-mercari-and-is-it-safe-and-legit/); [Incogni](https://blog.incogni.com/is-mercari-safe/)
- US fees (third-party, unverified): a flat 10% seller fee after a January 2025 restructure. — [closo.co 2026 guide](https://closo.co/blogs/blog/the-real-mercari-guide-japan-imports-seller-fees-and-avoiding-scams-in-2026)

**Carousell (Singapore / SE Asia)**
- **Verified badge**: the help page says it requires **Singpass + phone + email** and "does not provide complete assurance". The help page still mentions a green tick, while MHA and Carousell's 2025 report describe a **blue tick**. — [Carousell help: Verified Badge](https://support.carousell.com/hc/en-us/articles/360022767413--Singapore-What-is-a-Verified-Badge); [Carousell help: Singpass](https://support.carousell.com/hc/en-us/articles/360022583734--Singapore-How-does-SingPass-identity-verification-work); [MHA Carousell page](https://www.mha.gov.sg/e-commerce-marketplace-transaction-safety-ratings/carousell/)
- **Abuse of verified accounts**: scammers bought Singpass-verified accounts or used stolen credentials. Under the E-Commerce Code of the Online Criminal Harms Act, Carousell committed to new safeguards by **31 January 2026**: blocking Singpass IDs linked to suspended scam accounts, passkey and biometric login, and suspending accounts handed over to scammers. Since **April 2025** a Singpass ID can verify at most **2 accounts**. Reported scams on Carousell fell about **36%** year on year in April–June 2025. — [MHA press release](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/enhanced-verification-measures-by-carousell-under-the-e-commerce-code-of-the-online-criminal-harms-act/); [AsiaOne](https://www.asiaone.com/singapore/carousell-enhanced-verification-e-commerce-scams-mha)
- Carousell's Regional Scam Trends Report 2025 (published 28 April 2026) claims **over 99% scam-free transactions**. All **Professional** accounts must verify and carry a "Professional seller" badge. — [Carousell press](https://press.carousell.com/2026/04/28/carousells-regional-scam-trends-report-2025-records-over-99-scam-free-transactions-despite-evolving-scam-trends/)
- User sentiment: a HardwareZone forum thread says Singpass verification "can deter scammers" but "when report is made they do nothing". — [HardwareZone forum](https://forums.hardwarezone.com.sg/threads/carousell-now-use-singpass-to-verify-can-deter-scammers-key-issue-is-when-report-is-made-they-do-nothing.7120193/)

**Dubizzle / Bayut (UAE)**
- Dubizzle's **blue-tick "Badge of Trust"**: a seller uploads ID documents, a profile photo and a **5-second selfie video**, which staff review. Verified users get **priority customer support**. Verification was later extended with reviews and ratings, and with **Verified Business** accounts. — [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/business/technology/dubizzle-fights-against-uae-scammers-verifies-sellers-with-badge-of-trust); [Arabian Business 2](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/industries/technology/dubizzle-tackles-scam-with-verified-accounts-reviews-ratings); [Dubizzle help: Verified Business](https://support.dubizzle.com/hc/en-us/articles/22618206854290-Introducing-Verified-Business-on-dubizzle); [The National, 2023](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/paid-content/2023/08/24/innovative-new-verified-initiative-on-dubizzle-is-designed-to-enable-safer-selling/)
- "Verified by dubizzle" on property listings: staff check that a seller–agent agreement exists for the listing (dates from 2018). — [Dubizzle tools blog 2018](https://tools.dubizzle.com/blog/2018/03/01/verified-listings-faqs/); [Dubizzle verified properties](https://www.dubizzle.com/blog/property/dubizzle-verified-properties-feature/)

**Jiji (Nigeria, Kenya, Africa)**
- The **"Verified" store badge** comes from an **in-person visit by a Jiji offline agent**. An extra **"CAC Verified"** badge is added if the business registration (Corporate Affairs Commission) was uploaded during the visit. — [Jiji.ng FAQ 216](https://jiji.ng/faq/216)
- **"Verified ID"** matches an ID document to a selfie through **SmileID**. It is **available only to sellers with an active paid Boost package**, so verification is bundled with monetization. — [Jiji.co.ke FAQ 153](https://jiji.co.ke/faq/153); [Jiji.ng FAQ 125](https://jiji.ng/faq/125)

**Haraj (Saudi Arabia)**
- Commission model based on honesty: a **1% commission** per sale, buyer free, paid by bank transfer. Annual packages are an alternative (for example **SAR 2,390/year**, as of 2016). Commission discounts below 1% for car and real-estate sellers depend on timely payment, ad volume, complaints and sales volume. As of 2016 there was no online payment or escrow. Haraj turned down a $20M offer in 2016. — [Wamda, 2016](https://www.wamda.com/2016/05/ksa-car-seller-haraj-turns-down-20m)
- Browsing is possible **without registration**. Features include direct chat, filters and paid ad boosting. A recent App Store review calls the 1% "very fair". — [Google Play: Haraj](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.haraj.app&hl=en); [App Store SA](https://apps.apple.com/sa/app/%D8%AD%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%AC/id662557215); [DXB Apps blog](https://dxbapps.com/blog/why-is-haraj-so-popular-in-saudi-arabia)

### Inferences
- The strongest trust designs plug into national or ecosystem identity infrastructure: Singpass, Alipay/Zhima, and Japanese carrier networks for anonymous shipping. A Russian-market analogue would be Gosuslugi/ESIA verification plus a "safe deal" escrow with anonymous delivery codes. This is an inference, not a sourced finding.
- Badges degrade once they become a tradable asset. Carousell's countermeasures (a limit of 2 accounts per national ID, blocking IDs from banned accounts, passkey login) are a ready-made playbook.
- Jiji's model of tying verification to a paid boost and doing offline store visits suits low-trust, cash-heavy markets.

### Gaps
- Current Xianyu credit-level rules (鱼力值, seller credit tiers, 验货宝 inspection-escrow statistics) were not found. woshipm.com was blocked.
- The exact mechanics of Karrot's GPS neighborhood verification (radius, re-verification period, number of neighborhoods allowed) were not retrieved. From background knowledge (unverified): users verify by GPS that they are physically in the neighborhood, verification must be renewed periodically, and users choose a radius of nearby areas.
- Mercari Japan specifics (らくらくメルカリ便 / ゆうゆうメルカリ便 prices, the mutual-rating flow) and Jimoty features (free 0円 giveaways, local pickup) were not found in search.
- Bayut TruCheck and UAE PASS integration were not found.

## 3. Gamified and score-based reputation systems: how they are shown and computed

### Takeaway
Karrot's manner temperature is the standout gamified score. It uses a warm, human metaphor (body temperature 36.5 at start, 0 to 99 range), changes over time and weighs recent activity more. It gives users a single glanceable trust number on every profile and in chat, and it became part of the culture. It did not travel abroad and was reworked into a 30-based Karrot Score. Service platforms (58到家, UC) use hidden or semi-visible scores that drive dispatch and access.

### Cited Findings
- Karrot: starts at 36.5, range 0–99, built from ratings, reviews and sanctions, covers the last 12 months with recency weighting, decays with inactivity, and the formula is secret (see Q2). — [Newstap](https://www.newstap.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=184345); [Daangn community](https://www.daangn.com/kr/community/%EB%8B%B9%EA%B7%BC-%EB%A7%A4%EB%84%88-%EC%98%A8%EB%8F%84%EA%B0%80-36-5-%EB%B3%B4%EB%8B%A4-%EB%82%AE%EC%9D%80-%EA%B2%BD%EC%9A%B0%EB%8A%94-%EC%96%B4%EB%96%A4-%EA%B2%BD%EC%9A%B0%EC%9E%85%EB%8B%88%EA%B9%8C-8f6q9cxpvyvu/)
- Press in 2021 framed it as the reason users are "열광" (enthusiastic) about Karrot. — [Nate News 2021](https://news.nate.com/view/20210618n12520)
- Global version: Karrot Score with a base of 30. — [redbusbagman (search summary)](https://redbusbagman.com/karrotuxresearch/)
- 58到家: a worker service score on a 0–1 scale from order ratings, star levels (300 customers all rated 9 leads to 2-star), and dispatch weighted by satisfaction, rejection rate and willingness. — [CSDN](https://blog.csdn.net/qq_58446570/article/details/128684643); [高顿](https://m.gaodun.com/caiwu/557925.html); [21财经](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220318/herald/1b68ed9a9d31cab4b0bfbf3403151527.html)
- Urban Company advertises pros as "4.3+ rated", and the platform average was 4.79/5 (Q1 FY26). — [UC page](https://urbancompany.com/bangalore-refrigerator-repair-motappapalya-indiranagar); [Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/ipo/why-urban-company-nearly-74-on-listing-day-key-qualitative-factors-from-the-rhp/article-181426/)
- Haraj (2016): the commission discount works as a seller reputation lever, based on payment timeliness, complaints and volume. — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2016/05/ksa-car-seller-haraj-turns-down-20m)
- Xianyu: the Zhima score (600+ threshold) acts as an external reputation gate for prepayment. — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/1722103578625)

### Inferences
- Design pattern: a non-zero, "human" starting value (36.5) means new users look neutral rather than bad. Recency weighting and decay prevent banked reputation. A secret formula makes the score harder to game. Culturally specific metaphors need localization.
- A 4.79 average next to a 4.3 floor shows the usual rating inflation in 5-star systems. This is one reason Karrot used a different scale.

### Gaps
- Meituan merchant rating computation (for example, how 大众点评 star scores weight reviews) was not found. Xianyu's 鱼力值 or credit-tier display was not found.

## 4. Chat, offers, notifications, onboarding and community features; top complaints, app ratings and UX research

### Takeaway
Shared patterns include in-app chat as the default contact channel, browsing without registration (Haraj), local and community layers (Karrot's neighborhood feed; Xianyu's interest "fish ponds" according to background knowledge, not sourced here) and safety tooling (SOS buttons for workers at UC). Complaints cluster around three themes. Scams persist despite verification (Carousell, Karrot). Lead-gen directories produce spam and fake leads (Justdial, Sulekha). Workers object to pressure and fees on managed platforms (UC).

### Cited Findings
- Haraj: browse without registering, direct buyer–seller chat, advanced filters, paid boosting. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.haraj.app&hl=en); [DXB Apps](https://dxbapps.com/blog/why-is-haraj-so-popular-in-saudi-arabia)

### Inferences
- In service marketplaces, complaints move from buyers to workers once the platform standardizes. Any "instant" or quick-commerce feature needs worker-dignity and safety design from the start, or it risks UC-style PR backlash.
- Lead-gen models (paying for contacts) produce the most negative business-side sentiment. Pay-per-completed-job or commission (Haraj 1%, Xianyu 0.6%, capped) aligns incentives better.
